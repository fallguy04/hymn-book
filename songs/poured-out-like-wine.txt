Title: Poured Out Like Wine
Meter: Irregular
Source: writer unknown; NOT confirmed public domain. Added 10 Nov 2026 on Michael's decision to include camp songs whose writer can't be traced, knowing they may still be in copyright; remove at once if an owner asks. hymnary.org calls it "American folk hymn" and lists "© 1997 by Song Supplements, Inc." for the Praise for the Lord printing; earliest printing found is 1977, credited "Anon." Text as printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook (#32), with "Yes I'll so one" corrected to "Yes, I'll be so one" and the pronouns for the Lord capitalised. Suggested by Caleb L. Jenkins (#6).

Would you be poured out like wine upon the altar for Me?
Would you be broken like bread to feed the hungry?
Would you be so one with Me that you would do just as I will?
Would you be light, and life, and love, My word fulfilled?

Yes, I'll be poured out like wine upon the altar for You;
Yes, I'll be broken like bread to feed the hungry!
Yes, I'll be so one with You that I would do just as You will;
Yes, I'll be light, and life, and love, Your word fulfilled.
