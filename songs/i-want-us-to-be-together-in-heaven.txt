Title: I Want Us to Be Together in Heaven
Meter: Irregular
Source: writer unknown; NOT confirmed public domain. Added 10 Nov 2026 on Michael's decision to include camp songs whose writer can't be traced, knowing they may still be in copyright; remove at once if an owner asks. No text with this first line found; a widely recorded gospel song of the same title starts differently. Text as printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook (#20), with two misprints corrected: "one-day" to "one day", and "open us our eyes" to "open up our eyes" (a guess; check against how it's sung).

The love of God prepares a place,
No evil there, 'tis filled with grace;
	No need up there for worry or a falling tear.
All trials over in that land,
With trusting faith we'll understand;
	I want us to be together in Heaven.

Refrain:
I want us to be together in Heaven,
I want to walk down the streets of pure gold.
I want to run through the fields of green clover,
See the mansions, smell the flowers, hear the singing, it's all ours.
See the river gently flowing, feel the gentle breezes blowing,
I want us to be together in Heaven.

The gates of Heaven stand ajar,
Inviting all, both near and far;
	For one day God's great hand will close Heaven's door.
When we see Jesus from the skies,
Where will we open up our eyes?
	Forever lost, or all together in Heaven?

You may have a fancy car,
Big new house that shines by far;
	You may live to be a hundred years old;
But if you have not been saved,
It will end with the grave;
	But I want us to be together in Heaven.

You may be a millionaire,
Wearing clothes beyond compare;
	You may have the best that money can buy;
But if the blood was not applied,
Then in hell you'll lift your eyes;
	But I want us to be together in Heaven.
