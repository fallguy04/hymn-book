Title: More Love to Thee
Author: Elizabeth Payson Prentis, 1856
Meter: 6.4.6.4.6.6.4.4
Source: Open Hymnal Project (public domain). Text and line breaks reviewed 26 Aug 2026; not checked against the congregation's printed copy.

More love to Thee, O Christ;
	More love to Thee;
Hear Thou the pray'r I make
	On bended knee;
This is my earnest plea,
More love, O Christ, to Thee,
	More love to Thee;
More love to Thee.

Once earthly joy I craved,
	Sought peace and rest;
Now thee alone I seek,
	Give what is best;
This all my pray'r shall be,
More love, O Christ, to Thee,
	More love to Thee;
More love to Thee.

Then shall my latest breath,
	Whisper Thy praise,
This be the parting cry
	My heart shall raise;
This still its pray'r shall be,
More love, O Christ, to Thee,
	More love to Thee;
More love to Thee.
