Title: I Will Sing of the Mercies of the Lord Forever
Author: James H. Fillmore
Meter: Irregular
Source: public domain (1893). Text is Psalm 89:1. As printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook
Note: The camp book repeats stanza 1 after stanza 2.

I will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever,
	I will sing, I will sing.
I will sing of the mercies of the Lord forever,
	I will sing of the mercies of the Lord.

With my mouth will I make known
	Thy faithfulness, Thy faithfulness.
With my mouth will I make known
	Thy faithfulness to all generations.
