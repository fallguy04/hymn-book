Title: Alive, Alive
Meter: Irregular
Source: writer unknown; NOT confirmed public domain. Added 10 Nov 2026 on Michael's decision to include camp songs whose writer can't be traced, knowing they may still be in copyright; remove at once if an owner asks. No writer found on hymnary.org or elsewhere; earliest printing found is 2006 (arrangement © 2000, Group Publishing). Text as printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook (#1). Suggested by Michael (#9).

Alive, alive, alive forevermore,
	My Jesus is alive,
	Alive forevermore;
Alive, alive, alive forevermore,
	My Jesus is alive.

Sing hallelujah, sing hallelujah,
	My Jesus is alive forevermore;
Sing hallelujah, sing hallelujah,
	My Jesus is alive.
