Title: The Lord Is My Light
Author: George F. Root
Meter: Irregular
Source: public domain (1894; Root d. 1895). Text is Psalm 27. Words supplied by Michael Fall from the congregation's own singing.
Note: The refrain is sung twice through, with an "Amen" at the close.

The Lord is my light, is my light and my salvation;
	Whom shall I fear?
The Lord is the strength, is the strength of my heart;
	In whom shall I be afraid?

Though an host should encamp against me,
	My heart shall not fear;
Though war should rise against me,
	In this will I be confident.

One thing I have desired of the Lord,
	That I will seek after:

Refrain:
That I may dwell in the house of the Lord,
	That I may dwell,
	That I may dwell,
	That I may dwell,
	That I may dwell
All the days, all the days of my life.
