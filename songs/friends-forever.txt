Title: Friends Forever
Meter: Irregular
Source: writer unknown; NOT confirmed public domain. Added 10 Nov 2026 on Michael's decision to include camp songs whose writer can't be traced, knowing they may still be in copyright; remove at once if an owner asks. No match found on hymnary.org or elsewhere. Text as printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook (#11); the book runs the verses together, so the break between them is inferred from the rhymes.

Now it's time for us to say goodbye for just a while,
	So until we meet again, may God's love on you smile.
We'll be friends forever, and while we are apart,
	I will pray for you and always keep you in my heart.

Refrain:
And we'll be friends forever, Jesus and you and me;
	He ties our hearts together, and friends we'll always be.

When you see the sun, you'll know I see the same one too;
	When you see the stars, they'll say hello from me to you.
Thank You, God, for special friends we've grown to love so much;
	Many miles will come between, but we will keep in touch.
