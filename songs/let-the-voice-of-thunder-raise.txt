Title: Let the Voice of Thunder Raise
Meter: Irregular
Source: writer unknown; NOT confirmed public domain. Added 10 Nov 2026 on Michael's decision to include camp songs whose writer can't be traced, knowing they may still be in copyright; remove at once if an owner asks. No match found on hymnary.org or elsewhere. Text exactly as printed in the 2022 YFS Camp Songbook (#29), chorus first as the book has it. Several verse lines read like misprints ("To me on thee I call", "With nothing left, than righteous") but are left alone, since a guess would be worse; check against how it's sung.

Refrain:
Let the voice of thunder raise,
	Hallowed be to the God of my praise.
Heaven and earth shall stand amazed,
	Lifting their voice to the God of my praise.
Glory shall sing to the God of my praise.

O Lord, my rock, keep silent not;
	To me on Thee I call.
Let peace be known, to me I pray,
	Today display my glory.
O Lord of sight, of power and might,
	Delight Thyself in me.
With nothing left, than righteous,
	For all to see You live in me.
